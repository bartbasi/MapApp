Map label glyphs bundled with MapApp

What: Noto Sans Regular, Noto Sans Medium and Noto Sans Italic, as MapLibre
signed distance field glyph files (.pbf). Three Unicode ranges per style:
  0-255       Basic Latin and Latin-1 Supplement
  256-511     Latin Extended-A
  8192-8447   General Punctuation (dashes, curly quotes, ellipsis)
These cover United States English place and road names. A label character
outside them is drawn without that character.

Font: Noto Sans, Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors
(https://github.com/notofonts). Licensed under the SIL Open Font License,
Version 1.1. The license text is in OFL.txt in this folder.

Where the files came from: downloaded on 2026-11-12 from
https://protomaps.github.io/basemaps-assets/fonts/<font name>/<range>.pbf
(repository https://github.com/protomaps/basemaps-assets, commit
028c18f713baecad011301ff7a69acc39bcc2ae7). Those glyph files were made from
the Noto Sans fonts with https://github.com/maplibre/font-maker. OFL.txt is
that repository's fonts/OFL.txt. The files are unchanged. Only the folder names
were changed, to lower case with no spaces, so that the URLs need no escaping.

The app loads these files from its own bundle. It never fetches fonts from
the network.
